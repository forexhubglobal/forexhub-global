Moneta Funded

Moneta Funded is a proprietary trading firm (prop firm) that provides traders with access to company-funded capital. Instead of risking large amounts of personal funds, traders complete an evaluation challenge—or choose an Instant Funding program—to qualify for a funded trading account. Once funded, traders can trade the firm's capital while keeping a significant share of the profits.

Instant Funding

These programs are designed for both beginner and experienced traders looking to scale their trading careers.
